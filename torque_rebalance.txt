================================================================================
TORQUE REBALANCE - math and algorithm
b601_teleop / b601/assist.py :: TorqueRebalance          (designed 2026-08-26)
================================================================================

GOAL
  Isotropic drag feel: a hand force at the gripper should translate the EE the
  same way in every direction, instead of escaping as cheap wrist rotation.

NOTATION
  q  in R^6        joint positions            (joint1 .. joint6)
  qd in R^6        joint velocities (finite-difference estimate)
  Jv in R^{3x6}    position Jacobian of gripper_end, world frame:  v_ee = Jv qd
                   column Jv_i = direction & lever with which joint i moves the EE
  UP   = joints 1..3   (base / shoulder: "upstream",   indices [:3])
  DOWN = joints 4..6   (wrist:           "downstream", indices [3:])
  B = Jv[:, :3]        upstream columns
  W = Jv[:, 3:]        downstream columns
  o  = elementwise product;  lowpass_T(x_raw): x += (dt / (T + dt)) (x_raw - x)

PHYSICAL BACKGROUND (why the wrist "does everything")
  A hand force F at the EE loads EVERY joint at once through the transpose map
        tau_i = Jv_i . F                                  (virtual work: J^T F)
  Joint i yields only when |tau_i| exceeds its own breakaway friction brk_i.
  The chain yields at its weakest link: the wrist crosses its threshold at a
  lower push force (lower friction, and for radial pushes a bigger lever), the
  gripper then follows the hand, and the contact force plateaus BELOW the base
  joints' threshold. The base never sees enough torque, no matter the intent.
  Extra asymmetry while moving: qd_i = (Jv_i.F - fric_i) / kd_i, and the wrist's
  (lever/kd) slope is ~20x the shoulder's, so it supplies the hand's motion first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - MEASURE the external torque on the downstream joints
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  External torque is observable only through BACKDRIVE: a stuck joint hides it
  inside its static friction band, and in free-float drag (kp = 0) the motor
  does not resist a push, so motor-torque feedback cannot see it either.
  While a joint moves, the hand torque on it is what sustains the motion:

        tau_dn = fric_dn o tanh(qd_dn / v0) + kd_dn o qd_dn        in R^3
        tau_dn <- lowpass_{tau_f}(tau_dn)                          (zero at rest)

  fric_dn = [0.30, 0.21, 0.21] N.m  (Seeed friction sweep, j4/j5/j6)
  kd_dn   = the kd_drag actually commanded on j4..j6 (default 0.05)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - INFER the external torque on the upstream joints (Jacobian)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The upstream joints are the stuck ones, so their external torque cannot be
  measured; it is reconstructed. The hand force that explains the downstream
  torques is the damped least-squares solution of  W^T F = tau_dn :

        A      = W^T                          (3x3, rows = downstream joints)
        F_hat  = (A^T A + lambda^2 I)^-1 A^T tau_dn                in R^3
        tau_up = B^T F_hat                                         in R^3

  Observability caveat: only the force component in the span of the BACKDRIVING
  joints' lever rows is recoverable (minimum-norm elsewhere). One yielding wrist
  joint => 1-D information: e.g. a pure-outward push and an out-and-up push give
  identical j4 torque and are indistinguishable. This is why step 4 does NOT
  take its direction from F_hat.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - COMPARE the halves  ->  transfer gate w
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Wrong comparisons (both tried, both rejected):
   - raw torque vs raw torque: a joint yields against its OWN breakaway; for
     lateral pushes joint1 receives MORE raw torque than joint5 and still loses.
   - threshold-normalized loading vs loading: the step-2 ambiguity inflates the
     inferred upstream loading, and a loaded-but-stuck joint is not helping
     anyway - loading is not carriage.

  Robust comparison - what each half is actually DOING:

        r_down   = max_i |tau_dn_i| / brk_dn_i          ~1 while the wrist backdrives
        carry_up = |B qd_up| / (|B qd_up| + |W qd_dn| + eps)
                                                        upstream share of EE speed
        w        = clip(r_down, 0, 1) * (1 - carry_up)  in [0, 1]

  w ~ 1  : wrist is carrying the push alone            -> transfer fully
  w -> 0 : upstream half has taken over the motion     -> stop pushing it
  w = 0  : no hand at all                              -> no output

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - TRANSFER torque to the upstream joints (J^T size, J+ direction)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The two Jacobian maps answer different questions and can DISAGREE IN SIGN in
  skewed geometry (folded-wrist radial push: a force along the motion loads j2
  negative, but j2 must rotate positive to carry that motion):
        J^T F : torque the force EXERTS on a joint      (statics)
        J+ u  : rotation the joint needs to CARRY u     (kinematics)
  So: per-joint direction pattern from the motion-carry map, magnitude from the
  sensed force.

        u_m     = W qd_dn / |W qd_dn|          EE direction the wrist is carrying
        p       = B^T (B B^T + lambda^2 I)^-1 u_m       upstream rates carrying u_m
        F_equiv = (B B^T ... DLS) p            EE force the pattern p corresponds to
                  i.e. solve  B^T F_equiv = p  by damped least squares
        s       = gain * |F_hat| / |F_equiv|   so the pattern's EE force
                                               equals gain x the sensed hand force
        tau_tx  = clip( lowpass_{tau_f}( w * s * p ), +-tau_max )   per joint 1..3

  Injected into the drag torque:  tau_cmd = g(q) + fric_comp o tanh(qd/0.08) + tau_tx

SAFETY PROPERTIES (all tested in tests/test_assist.py)
  - zero at rest: no backdrive => tau_dn = 0, w = 0, u_m undefined => no output
  - bounded: |EE-equivalent force| <= gain x sensed hand force; per-joint tau_max
    cap; controller's per-joint clamp and vel_abort freeze remain as backstops
  - self-fading: gate closes as the upstream half takes the motion over, and the
    u_m source vanishes when the wrist stops slipping
  - no wrong-way pressing: every driven joint aids the observed motion (J+ sign)
  - no rate targets, no integrator, no added damping

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PSEUDOCODE (per control cycle, 100 Hz, DRAG phase only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  state: tau_dn = 0 (R^3), tau_tx = 0 (R^3)     # low-pass filter states

  update(q, qd, dt):
      Jv = jacobian(q)[:3]                      # 3x6
      B, W = Jv[:, :3], Jv[:, 3:]

      # step 1: measure downstream external torque from backdrive
      raw    = fric_dn * tanh(qd[3:] / v0) + kd_dn * qd[3:]
      tau_dn = lowpass(tau_dn, raw, tau_f, dt)

      # step 2: infer hand force and upstream received torque
      A      = W.T
      F_hat  = solve(A.T A + lam2*I3, A.T tau_dn)
      tau_up = B.T @ F_hat                      # displayed; sizing input

      # step 3: compare halves -> gate
      r_down   = max(|tau_dn| / brk_dn)
      carry_up = |B qd[:3]| / (|B qd[:3]| + |W qd[3:]| + eps)
      w        = clip(r_down, 0, 1) * (1 - carry_up)

      # step 4: transfer (J^T size, J+ direction)
      v_dn = W @ qd[3:]
      if |v_dn| < 1e-4:  raw_tx = 0
      else:
          u_m     = v_dn / |v_dn|
          p       = B.T @ solve(B B.T + lam2*I3, u_m)
          F_equiv = solve(A_up.T A_up + lam2*I3, A_up.T p)   with A_up = B.T
          s       = gain * |F_hat| / |F_equiv|
          raw_tx  = w * s * p
      tau_tx = clip(lowpass(tau_tx, raw_tx, tau_f, dt), -tau_max, tau_max)

      return [tau_tx, 0, 0, 0]                  # torque on joints 1..3 only

PARAMETERS (defaults)
  gain     = 2.0     force multiplication wrist -> base (--assist-gain)
  tau_max  = 1.2     N.m cap per upstream joint (the --assist TAU argument)
  fric_dn  = [0.30, 0.21, 0.21] N.m   wrist Coulomb friction (Seeed sweep)
  brk_dn   = fric_dn                  downstream breakaway (same table)
  kd_dn    = [0.05, 0.05, 0.05]       wrist kd_drag actually commanded
  v0       = 0.08    rad/s   tanh knee (above finite-difference velocity noise)
  tau_f    = 0.1     s       low-pass time constant (measurement and output)
  lambda   = 0.05    m       damping of every least-squares solve (lam2 = 0.0025)

RESULTS (sim, identical 2 N pushes at the folded-wrist pose q = [0,1.08,1.22,-1,0,0])
  EE travel      plain      rebalanced
  lateral        0.263 m    0.269 m
  radial         0.085 m    0.148 m     (the joint4 direction: +74%)
  vertical       0.233 m    0.233 m
  direction spread 3.1x -> 1.8x; high-stiction j1 (0.8 N.m) recruited with peak
  0.49 N.m transfer, zero torque sign flips, stops with the hand.

RUN
  python scripts/gravity_drag.py drag --assist 1.2 [--assist-gain 2]
  status line:  tau_dn <j4 j5 j6>  ->  tau_up <j1 j2 j3>   w <gate>   tx <j1 j2 j3>
